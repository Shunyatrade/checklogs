Overview

The RajOC (Dwarkadhish OC Variant) EA is a derivative of the RajONE scalping and level progression strategy. It inherits the full ZigZag pivot detection on M15, SafeNet trail management, and momentum-based exits — but introduces a Level 21 Cap that resets the progression counter back to Level 1 once a trade closes at level 21 or higher.

This variant is designed for capital preservation: when the virtual balance grows past the level 21 threshold, the EA locks the accumulated profits, resets v_balance to $20, and restarts the cycle. Only the $20 virtual float is used for position sizing — the locked balance is never touched again.

Core Strategy Mechanics

Below are the structural pillars that define how the EA manages risk exposure dynamically.

Level Progression System

Tracks progress through a virtual ledger (v_balance), mapping values to indices from 1 to 30. Each index unlocks specific lots (lots[]) and requires profit goals (goals[]) before progressing to the next stage.

Safe Net SL/TP Strategy

Secures profits dynamically. Once a trade gains BE_Trigger pips, the SL shifts to lock in BE_Profit. Beyond Trail_Trigger, a Trailing Stop tracks price from a set distance (Trail_Dist).

Stepdown & Recovery

On a loss, the system immediately drops the progression index by 1, flags skip_next = true to skip the next immediate trade, and forces a minimum safety volume of 0.01 lots if balance falls below $20.

Level 21 Cap — OC Only

When a trade closes at level 21 or higher, the EA resets c_level to 1 and v_balance to $20. All profits accumulated up to that point are "locked" — the EA restarts fresh with only $20 virtual balance for position sizing.
